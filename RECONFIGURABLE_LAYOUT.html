Routing-only example on the maintained 16-port FP32 BIRRD+ topology. Pass and Swap preserve values; reduction modes are not used. Output port p writes bank p at row 0. The bank grid wraps for display.

Commands are generated and checked with the repository's BIRRD compiler. Fixed wiring also permutes lanes, so all-Pass does not mean identity. The animation slows the transfers for teaching; it is not an RTL timing replay.
